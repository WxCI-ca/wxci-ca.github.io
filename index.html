The technology

One stack, from sensor to governed decision.

The BorealWave stack is a neuro-symbolic decision engine: neural perception handles noisy real-world signals; a symbolic policy layer makes the call — every output explainable, versioned, and remotely governable.

WaveCore — the compression engine

The same wavelet mathematics targets the models themselves: weights stored in the wavelet domain (.bwave format, implemented and verified with a 93-check round-trip suite), reconstructed layer-by-layer in integer-only arithmetic. Large-model compression payoff (2–4× targets) is our Stage-2 research objective.
